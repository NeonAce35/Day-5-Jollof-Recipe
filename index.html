Simple Jollof Rice Recipe

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, onions, and a variety of spices. It is often served with chicken, fish, or beef. Here is a simple recipe to make Jollof rice at home.

Preparation Time

  • Total: Approximately 65 minutes
  • Preparation: 20 minutess
  • Cook: 45 minutess

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 5 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 large onion
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 bay leaves (optional)

Instructions

  • Wash the rice: Rinse the rice thoroughly with clean water and set aside.
  • Dice the vegetables: Chop the tomatoes, bell peppers, and onion into small pieces.
  • Heat the oil: In a large pot, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat.
  • Add the onions: Sauté the onions until they are translucent.
  • Add the peppers and tomatoes: Add the red bell peppers and tomatoes, and cook for a few minutes until they start to soften.
  • Add the tomato paste: Stir in the tomato paste and cook for another minute.
  • Add the spices: Add the curry powder, dried thyme, seasoning cubes, and salt. Mix well.
  • Add the chicken stock: Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil.
  • Add the rice: Stir in the rinsed rice and reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 45 minutes or until the rice is cooked and tender.
